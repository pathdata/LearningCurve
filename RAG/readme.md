## Retrieval augmented Generation and its evaluation

Metric Answers                                                                     

Precision@k  Of what I retrieved, how much was relevant?                                                         
Recall@k     Of what's relevant, how much did I retrieve?                                                         
MRR          How fast did I find the first good one? (Mean Reciprocal Rank)                                                            
nDCG         How well-ordered is the whole list, not just the top hit? (normalised Discounted Cumulative Gain)
